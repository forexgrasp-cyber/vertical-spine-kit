Frequently asked questions

How does the voice agent work?

When a customer calls your Twilio number, our webhook handles the call: plays a consent prompt, gathers speech, extracts intent using LLM, queries your knowledge base, and responds with a natural-sounding answer.

Can I customize the knowledge base?

Yes! Ingest services (JSON), pricing (CSV), and FAQs (Markdown) per tenant. The RAG system retrieves the top-3 most relevant chunks for every query.

What tiers are supported?

Voice agent requires Starter+ tier. Free tier is denied with a friendly message. Upgrade via Stripe checkout or subscription - entitlements are granted automatically via webhook.

Is it compliant?

Yes. Consent is captured and written to an immutable hash-chained audit log. Recordings are purged after 30 days. PII (phone, address) is encrypted at rest.
